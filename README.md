**Website Feedback**
Talk to at least 5 different people you know to get feedback on your website. Your website does not need to be finished, and you may find it helpful to talk to different people during various stages of the development process.

**Person 1 - Isaac Hamm, Software Engineer at InComm Payments**

1 - Simplify. In general, there's just a lot of different things. Multiple different fonts, and all sorts of colors. Consistency among fonts and simplicity among colors -- choose one or two and really stick with them throughout! Your colors for the logo are great, but you've got a lot of other blues as sort of "accent colors". Having the one blue with some neutral accent tones (grays, blacks, whites) I think would make the logo colors pop more. Another factor with color is that higher contrast is better -- The black on the mid-royal blue doesn't quite pop as hard as I'd like it to.
2 - On the timeline page, you've got the buttons for the numbers and the "top" button that float over everything. I like having them accessible at all times! But the "top" one is unnecessary when I'm already at the top -- you could have it switch from "bottom" to "top" depending where you're at, or just have it disappear when you're at the top. You could also have it be "most recent" to "oldest" as an alternative description. As for the numbers, for me they just feel a little out of place in the bottom left corner. Maybe put them closer to the top button? Or have them on the far right side and highlight whichever one is current? Sort of like code documentation does. Angular's documentation does something similar: https://angular.dev/guide/components/selectors
3 - You could shrink the header at scroll but still have it available at the top at all times so I can hit the home button at any time.
4 - Could make the timeline links an openable/closeable menu.
5 - Could make each event an accordion event, like this: https://material.angular.dev/components/expansion/overview. You don't have to make only one openable at a time, but it would be an option to help scrollability! (Just cause the page is a little long to scroll through)
6 - The timeline link's text also jumps when you hover over it. Layout shift is usually a good indication of usability/accessibility -- meaning that stuff should jump around as little as possible. See https://web.dev/articles/cls for more info. Similarly, I like the CSS animations, but it is a little busy on first load -- just my preference!

Overall, it's a very cool website. I'm just a picky web dev because I do this all day haha. But keep building! We love cool, personal, passionate websites! I'm currently revamping mine and could use some feedback when I'm done with it if you're open to it.
